Classic Fudgy Brownie

Ingredients:

  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/3 cup cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Steps:

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a 9x9 inch pan.
  2. Melt butter and mix with sugar, eggs, and vanilla.
  3. Stir in cocoa, flour, and salt until combined.
  4. Pour batter into the pan and bake for 20-25 minutes.
  5. Cool before cutting into squares.

Walnut Crunch Brownie

Ingredients:

  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/3 cup cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup chopped walnuts

Steps:

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a pan.
  2. Melt butter, mix with sugar, eggs, and vanilla.
  3. Add cocoa, flour, salt, then fold in walnuts.
  4. Bake for 22-27 minutes.
  5. Let cool, then slice and serve.

Peanut Butter Swirl Brownie

Ingredients:

  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/3 cup cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/3 cup peanut butter

Steps:

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a pan.
  2. Melt butter, mix with sugar, eggs, and vanilla.
  3. Add cocoa, flour, and salt and stir well.
  4. Pour half batter into pan. Drop peanut butter by spoonfuls and swirl.
  5. Pour remaining batter on top and bake 25 minutes.

Mint Chocolate Brownie

Ingredients:

  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/3 cup cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 tsp peppermint extract

Steps:

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease pan.
  2. Melt butter, mix with sugar, eggs, vanilla, and peppermint extract.
  3. Add cocoa, flour, and salt, mix well.
  4. Bake for 20-25 minutes.
  5. Cool and enjoy minty brownies!

Blondie Brownie

Ingredients:

  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup white chocolate chips

Steps:

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease pan.
  2. Melt butter, mix with brown sugar, eggs, and vanilla.
  3. Add flour, baking powder, and salt; mix well.
  4. Stir in white chocolate chips.
  5. Bake 20-25 minutes. Cool and cut.
